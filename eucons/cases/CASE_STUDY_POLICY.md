# EUCONS Case Study Publication Policy v1

## Purpose

Case studies are proof products, not synthetic marketing stories. A public case must be reconstructible from E03 publishable claims and must pass an explicit confidentiality decision before EUCONS may use it as evidence of Euroconsult experience or results.

## Public case gate

A case study may be `PUBLISHABLE` only when:

1. it has at least one `PUBLISHABLE` E03 `PROJECT_RESULT` claim;
2. each stated outcome, including every number, percentage, duration, status or other material result, is traceable to one of those result claims;
3. the case has a confidentiality mode of `PUBLIC_APPROVED` or `ANONYMIZED_PUBLIC`;
4. a named client requires a `PUBLISHABLE` `CLIENT_RELATIONSHIP` claim and `PUBLIC_APPROVED` confidentiality;
5. an anonymized case exposes no client name, legal identifier, contact details or other direct identifier;
6. any testimonial requires a `PUBLISHABLE` `TESTIMONIAL` claim, explicit testimonial consent and `PUBLIC_APPROVED` confidentiality;
7. service associations point only to E02 services;
8. people associations point only to E04 publishable people;
9. project/funding facts used to describe a programme, deadline, budget or eligibility remain governed by their own E03/E09 evidence requirements and are not inferred from the case narrative.

If any gate is incomplete or contradictory, the case remains `HOLD`.

## Confidentiality modes

- `PRIVATE` — not eligible for public projection.
- `PUBLIC_APPROVED` — client identity and approved facts may be public within the exact evidence/permission scope.
- `ANONYMIZED_PUBLIC` — the case may be public only after direct identifiers and disallowed indirect identifiers are removed.

Confidentiality approval is not inferred from the fact that a project or beneficiary is otherwise publicly discoverable.

## Outcome discipline

A case may summarize an evidenced result but cannot improve it. No invented grant value, project budget, success rate, number of projects, savings, jobs, score, ranking, delivery time or other performance metric is allowed. Outcome claims that are stale, disputed or superseded remain non-public until resolved.

## Development fallback

Until Euroconsult-specific client/project evidence and publication permissions pass E03, the canonical public case projection may be empty. EUCONS must omit case proof rather than display demo clients, fictional success stories or unverified numbers. Later E08 presentation cannot claim case-study proof unless at least one E05 case has actually passed this policy.
