# EUCONS Product Canon v1

## Mission

EUCONS.ro is Euroconsult's commercial digital operating system, not a brochure site.

Its primary loop is:

verified expertise/opportunity -> useful public content -> qualified visitor -> structured lead -> funding/service match -> proposal -> follow-up -> client -> evidence-backed case study.

## Primary audiences

1. Companies and entrepreneurs seeking investment or EU-funding support.
2. Public authorities and public institutions.
3. NGOs and other eligible organizations.
4. Existing beneficiaries needing implementation, reporting, procurement or project-recovery support.

## Public product surfaces

- Homepage and service discovery.
- Service catalogue.
- Funding opportunities relevant to commercial audiences.
- Team/expert profiles.
- Evidence-backed case studies.
- Guides, analyses, FAQs and good practices.
- Project evaluation and proposal request journeys.
- Contact and legal/privacy pages.

## Commercial products

- Lead Engine.
- Opportunity Matching Engine.
- CRM Lite.
- Offer Engine.
- Editorial/Authority Engine.
- LinkedIn and Facebook distribution adapters.
- Commercial email/follow-up adapter.
- Analytics and attribution contract.

## Hard truth rules

EUCONS must never invent:

- funding status, deadline, budget or eligibility;
- Euroconsult clients, contracts, project results or testimonials;
- expert biographies or credentials;
- prices, discounts or binding commercial terms;
- consent to marketing contact.

Missing or contradictory material facts produce `HOLD`, not publication or dispatch.

## Separation from sibling products

PARTENER.EU is funding intelligence. EUCONS may consume only verified public/commercial projections and may not mutate PARTENER canonical dossiers.

VÂLCEA CLAR is a local-news product. EUCONS may reuse generic CIVORA infrastructure patterns but no editorial content, branding or local-news assumptions.

## Terminal acceptance

Development reaches `PRODUCTION_READY` only when an end-to-end dry-run proves:

verified opportunity -> EUCONS projection -> site page -> prospect profile -> match -> qualified lead -> service recommendation -> offer artifact -> email/social outboxes -> receipts -> analytics events -> deterministic recovery.

External ownership actions such as DNS/hosting credentials and LinkedIn/Facebook authorization are explicitly outside the development closure gate and belong to final handoff.
