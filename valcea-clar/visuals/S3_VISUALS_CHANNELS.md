# VÂLCEA CLAR — S3 Visuals & Channels

S3 resolves the gap between a verified story and its visual/channel presentation.

## Model

1. Prefer an approved story-specific real photograph when one exists.
2. If a photograph would be generic, misleading or unavailable, use an **original editorial card** built only from the verified story text/data.
3. The card is explicitly labelled as editorial graphics and has `depicts_real_scene=false`.
4. Facebook may use the canonical story link whose OpenGraph image is the S3 card.
5. Instagram uses a channel-native verified fact card.
6. Threads remains text-first.
7. TikTok stays on hold until current subject/event media is eligible; an editorial card does not masquerade as current-event footage.

The initial S3 pilot covers the eight pilot stories, including the three S2 acceptance forms (straight news, explainer and service news).

## Acceptance

S3 closes when:
- each pilot story has a canonical site/OpenGraph visual;
- visual provenance is explicit and machine-checkable;
- the site story renderer exposes the same canonical visual in article metadata;
- channel plans distinguish photo, editorial-card, text-first and hold states;
- TikTok cannot be unlocked by an editorial card alone.
