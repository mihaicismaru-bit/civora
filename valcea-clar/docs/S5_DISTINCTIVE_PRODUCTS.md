# VÂLCEA CLAR — S5 Distinctive Products

S5 makes the publication visibly different through three reader products, all derived from already verified canonical material.

## 1. Clarificăm / Verificat din date

Public route: `/clarificam/`.

The pilot contains:
- the Călimănești traffic-restriction story, structured as documented facts vs. limits;
- the August unemployment explainer, structured as published data, reproducible calculations and limits of interpretation.

The product never assigns a true/false score and never upgrades evidence beyond the source story.

## 2. Unde ieșim — verificat editorial

Public route: `/unde-iesim/verificat/`.

Only `DRAFT_ELIGIBLE` rows from the canonical `web/unde-iesim.json` dataset appear. The page is explicitly not a ranking. It shows verification basis, last verification time, useful known fields and unresolved fields.

## 3. Dosare

Public route: `/dosare/`.

The first pilot is the already-published Open Air Blues Brezoi 2026 dossier. S5 creates an index/landing product around selected, already-published dossier paragraphs and links back to the canonical story with its complete source list. No new claim is created in the landing page.

## Persistence

S5 is invoked from S4, so canonical UX rebuilds regenerate these products. The homepage receives a three-card product module. S5 does not write the story archive, fact kernel or source datasets.
